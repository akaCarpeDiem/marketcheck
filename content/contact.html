How to reach Market Check on marketcheck.money.

For questions, feedback, a wrong number, or anything about privacy or these policies, email:

support@marketcheck.money

Reporting a wrong price

It helps to include the symbol, what the page showed, what you expected and roughly when you saw it. The sources and methods page explains where each figure comes from and how each change is measured, which answers many questions about small differences from a broker's screen.

Your account and your data

If you signed in, you can delete everything saved for your account yourself: open the account menu at the top right and choose Delete my saved data. You can also ask by email from the address you sign in with. The privacy policy describes what is stored.

Sign-in emails

Sign-in codes are sent from a no-reply address. That mailbox is not read, so please write to support@marketcheck.money instead.
