About Market Check

Official site: marketcheck.money. A free market dashboard, not a brokerage.

Market Check puts the markets most people follow on one page: crypto, US stocks and funds, the major indexes, gold, silver and oil, and the headlines moving them. It is built for the quick look you take several times a day, and it stays out of the way while you take it.

What you can do here

  • See what is moving. The biggest gainers and losers across crypto, Solana and Ethereum tokens, stocks and funds, over the last hour, day, week, month or year.
  • Read the market at a glance. The heat map draws every coin, stock or fund as a bubble or tile, sized by market value or by how far it moved.
  • Follow your own list. Add anything to your watchlist, or enter what you hold to see its live value, today's change and your profit or loss.
  • Make the front page yours. Sign in to choose the six tiles at the top, the indexes underneath and what each movers panel shows. Your choices follow you to any device.
  • Catch up quickly. A short brief of the day, what changed since your last visit, and the dates ahead: market holidays, early closes and Federal Reserve decisions.
  • Browse the full tables. The markets page lists the 250 largest cryptocurrencies, more than 100 large US stocks and 50 major funds, sortable by any column.
  • Read the news. Headlines from major publishers in ten topics, from markets and investing to real estate, policy and commodities, each linking to the original story.
  • Find something to listen to. The podcast hub is a hand-picked directory of shows on money, business and technology, with the newest episode of each.
  • Play the daily call. Guess whether the S&P 500 closes higher or lower, keep a streak, and see how you rank on the leaderboard.

Where the numbers come from

Prices come from public market data services: CoinGecko for crypto, Yahoo Finance for stocks, funds, indexes and commodities, and Jupiter for Solana tokens. Headlines come from publishers' public RSS feeds. The sources and methods page lists every source, how often each one refreshes and exactly how each change is measured.

Every figure can be delayed, incomplete or wrong compared with a broker or an exchange. Check a price with your broker before you act on it.

What Market Check is not

Market Check on marketcheck.money is a free market dashboard. It is not MarketCheck the vehicle listings company, and not a brokerage, exchange, bank or investment adviser.

It does not place orders, hold money or give personal recommendations. Nothing on this site is financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Free to use

Everything works without an account. Signing in is optional and only adds saving your lists and layout across devices. The site is paid for by advertising, which is always labelled and kept below the first screen.
