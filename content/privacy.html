Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026. Official site: marketcheck.money (Market Check).

In short

  • You can use Market Check without an account. Your watchlist, portfolio and settings then stay in your own browser.
  • If you choose to sign in, we store your email address, your name and what you save, so it is there on your other devices.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not connect to any brokerage, bank or wallet. Holdings are numbers you type in.
  • The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and Google AdSense to show advertising. Both use cookies.
  • You can delete everything saved for your account at any time from the account menu.

What this site is

Market Check on marketcheck.money is a free dashboard of market prices and headlines. It is not a brokerage, bank or investment adviser.

Using the site without an account

No account is needed. Your watchlist, portfolio, daily call record, display choices and the time of your last visit are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. You can sign in with Google, with Microsoft, or with a one-time code sent to your email address.

What we receive

  • Google or Microsoft: your email address, your name, your account's identifier with that provider and, from Google, the address of your profile picture. We never see your password, and we do not get access to your mail, contacts or files.
  • Email code: the email address you type. The code is delivered by our email provider, Resend. A scrambled (hashed) copy of the code and a count of recent requests are kept for up to 15 minutes to check the code and prevent abuse, then deleted automatically.

What we store for your account

  • Your watchlist and the holdings you enter (symbol, quantity and, if you give it, the price you paid).
  • Your layout: the tiles, indexes and movers panels you chose.
  • Your daily call record and a few display settings.

This is stored with our hosting provider, Cloudflare, under your account identifier, and is used only to show your own lists back to you. A copy is also kept in your browser so the page loads quickly.

Staying signed in

When you sign in we set one cookie, mc_session, that keeps you signed in for about 30 days. It contains your email address, name and account identifier, is signed so it cannot be altered, and is readable only by this site. A second cookie that lasts ten minutes is used during Google or Microsoft sign-in to protect the sign-in itself. Signing out removes the session cookie.

Deleting your data

Open the account menu at the top right and choose Delete my saved data. This removes everything stored for your account and signs you out. You can also ask us to do it by writing to support@marketcheck.money from the address you sign in with.

Hosting (Cloudflare)

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare automatically processes standard request details such as IP address, browser type, time and the address requested, for security, reliability and abuse prevention. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for how it handles that data.

Price, logo and news providers

Most prices and all headlines are fetched by our server, so those providers do not see you. A few things are loaded by your browser directly from other companies, which means they receive your IP address and browser type as with any web request:

  • Price history for a coin's chart, from CoinGecko, and the list of coin prices itself at moments when our server cannot get it.
  • Coin, token and company logos, from CoinGecko, Financial Modeling Prep and token issuers' own image hosts.
  • Thumbnail pictures next to headlines, from the publisher or video site.
  • Your profile picture, from Google, if you signed in with Google.

Following a link to a publisher, exchange or other site takes you to that site, under its own privacy policy. See sources and methods for the full list of sources.

Advertising

Market Check uses Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-4059575347806121) to show advertising, and publishes an ads.txt file authorizing Google as a direct seller. Advertisements are labelled and placed below the first screen.

Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, and may show ads based on your earlier visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings, and read more in how Google uses advertising cookies.

Analytics

Market Check uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used: which pages are viewed, roughly where visits come from, and what kind of device and browser are used. Google Analytics sets cookies (for example _ga) and collects the page address, the referring page, approximate location and device information. We look at this in total only, to measure traffic and improve the site. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not send your account details, watchlist or holdings to Google Analytics.

Google processes this data under its own policies. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and the Google privacy policy. You can opt out by blocking cookies in your browser or by installing the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

How we use information

  • To run the dashboard and show your saved lists and layout back to you.
  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To find and fix outages and prevent abuse.
  • To measure overall traffic with Google Analytics.
  • To show advertising through Google AdSense.
  • To reply when you write to us.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not send marketing email. The only email we send is the sign-in code you ask for.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete it. Sign-in codes and request counts expire within 15 minutes. The session cookie expires after about 30 days. Messages you send us are kept as long as needed to deal with them.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site, its hosting, its advertising or its analytics change. The date at the top changes when we do.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@marketcheck.money, or see the contact page.
