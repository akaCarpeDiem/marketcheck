Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 · Official site: marketcheck.money (Market Check). A *.pages.dev hostname may appear as a Cloudflare Pages hosting alias only.

What this site is

Market Check on marketcheck.money is a free public dashboard of market prices and headlines (crypto, equities, commodities, and public RSS news). It is not a brokerage, bank, or investment advisor.

No accounts

Market Check does not require an account. There is no sign-up wall for the public dashboard. You can use the site without creating a login.

Browser storage (localStorage)

The site uses your browser’s localStorage only for UI preferences and your watchlist on this device. That data stays in your browser unless you clear site data. We do not sync watchlists to a server for the public checker.

Hosting logs (Cloudflare)

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may automatically collect standard request metadata (for example IP address, user agent, timestamps, and requested URLs) for security, reliability, caching, and abuse prevention. See Cloudflare’s own documentation for details of their processing.

Third-party quote and news providers

Prices and headlines are requested from third-party public APIs and RSS feeds (for example CoinGecko, Yahoo Finance chart/quote endpoints, and news publishers’ RSS). Those providers have their own privacy practices. Market Check does not control their servers or policies. See Sources & methodology.

Advertising

Market Check uses Google AdSense (publisher client ca-pub-4059575347806121) to display advertising. The AdSense script is included in page HTML, and this site publishes an ads.txt file authorizing Google as a direct seller. Ad placements are reserved below the fold only (nothing above the first quote row). Reserved, clearly labeled advertisement slots may appear until individual ad-unit slot IDs are configured.

Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Learn more at Google’s Advertising policies and Ads Settings.

Analytics

Market Check uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed, roughly where visits come from, and what kind of device and browser are used. Google Analytics sets cookies (for example _ga) and collects usage data like the page address, referring page, approximate location, and device information. We use this data only in aggregate to measure traffic and improve the site; it is not used to identify you personally, and we do not combine it with any account information (the dashboard has no accounts).

Google processes this data under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can opt out by blocking cookies in your browser or installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

How we use information

  • Operate and improve the dashboard
  • Diagnose outages and abuse via hosting logs
  • Measure aggregate site traffic with Google Analytics
  • Respond to contact messages you send
  • Display Google AdSense advertising as described above

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product, hosting, ads, or analytics setup changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
