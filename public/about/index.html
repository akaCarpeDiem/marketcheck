About Market Check

Official site: marketcheck.money · public market dashboard. A *.pages.dev hostname may appear as a Cloudflare Pages hosting alias only.

What it shows

Market Check is a free, public dashboard that displays market prices and related public information: crypto assets, US equities / ETFs / indexes, commodities, and news headlines. The live UI is a dense, numbers-first board — useful for a quick glance, not for placing trades.

Where the data comes from

  • Crypto — primarily CoinGecko (and related public market APIs)
  • Equities, ETFs, indexes, commodities — Yahoo Finance chart / quote endpoints
  • Headlines — public RSS feeds from news publishers (no affiliation implied)

All figures can be delayed, incomplete, or wrong relative to any broker or exchange. Upstream providers have their own terms and availability limits. Full refresh intervals, stale/error rules, and market-hours caveats: Sources & methodology.

What it is not

Market Check on marketcheck.money is a free market quotes dashboard. It is not MarketCheck the car-listing / vehicle marketplace company, and not a brokerage, exchange, investment advisor, bank, or trading platform.

It does not execute orders, hold funds, or give personalized recommendations. Nothing on this site is financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Free to use; no account required.
